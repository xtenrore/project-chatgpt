Give ChatGPT the job. Keep the state outside the turn.

Project Supervisor keeps a durable job ledger in your browser extension, watches the real ChatGPT Project tab, detects stalls and limits, and continues only when it is safe to do so. Closing this dashboard or your phone does not stop the extension.
